# Notes — Market Requirements Document

## Market problem

Most note-taking apps optimize for breadth — sync, collaboration, rich formatting — which adds signup and setup steps for the common case of just wanting to jot something down quickly. There's a small but real niche for a tool that skips all of that.

## Target market

Individuals who want a fast, local, no-account scratchpad rather than a full note-taking platform. Not a competitor to full-featured tools like Notion or Evernote — a lighter-weight alternative for a narrower job.

## Why now

This is a small utility-scale project rather than a market-timed product bet; its value is in solving the zero-friction note-taking need directly, on demand, at any time.

## Competitive landscape

- **Full-featured note platforms (Notion, Evernote, etc.)** — powerful but require an account and have real onboarding overhead for a quick note
- **OS-native notes apps** — fast and local, but tied to a specific device/OS ecosystem
- **Plain text files / browser bookmarklets** — zero overhead but no organization or search

## Value proposition

A note tool that opens instantly, requires no account, and keeps notes searchable and organized locally — positioned between "no tool at all" and a full account-based note platform.

## Positioning

A minimal, no-account utility for quick notes — not intended to compete with full-featured, sync-enabled note platforms.

## Status

Live as a small self-contained utility project; not a commercial product.
